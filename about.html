We Believe Every Great Story Deserves a Great Book

Meridian Publishing House was founded to give first-time authors the same caliber of writing, editing, and publishing support that traditional publishers reserve for their biggest names.

Our Story

Founded By Writers, For Authors

We started Meridian after seeing too many brilliant ideas — from founders, survivors, experts, and storytellers — die in a Google Doc because their authors didn't have time to write, or didn't know how to publish.

Today, our team of vetted ghostwriters, editors, designers, and publishing specialists has helped more than 420 authors turn an idea into a finished, professionally published book.
