Our position

We are not a publisher.
We work for you.

A traditional publisher buys your rights and pays you a royalty. A vanity press charges you and keeps a share of sales. We do neither. You pay us for professional work, once, and then the book is entirely yours — copyright, ISBN, retail accounts and every cent it earns.

That arrangement also means our incentive is simple. We are not hoping your book sells enough to recoup an advance. We are hoping you recommend us to the next author, which only happens if the book is good.

Your voice, not ours

Every project begins with interviews, and a voice sample you approve before drafting starts.

Honest scoping

We regularly tell authors their idea is too broad, or the book should be shorter. Before they pay, not after.

Total confidentiality

Everyone who touches a manuscript signs an NDA. We are never credited and never name clients without permission.

Built to be read

Covers, metadata and categories decided against live retail data, because a finished book nobody finds is only half the job.
