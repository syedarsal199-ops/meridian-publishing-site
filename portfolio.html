Case study

The Long Road Home

Renee arrived with two decades of journals and no idea how to shape them. Fourteen weeks and twenty-two interviews later, she had a finished memoir.

MemoirThe Long Road HomeRenee Alston

“I sent my ghostwriter a box of old journals and got back the book I had been trying to write for a decade. It sounds exactly like me — just clearer.”

The challenge was selection. Renee had material covering forty years; the book needed one spine. Her writer identified the thread — a return to a town she had spent her twenties escaping — and built a three-act structure around it, cutting roughly two thirds of the raw material.
