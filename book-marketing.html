Book Marketing & Launch

Publishing a book and selling a book are different projects. The first ends when the file uploads. The second is where most self-published books quietly stop.

The approach

We would rather sell a thousand copies to the right readers than chase a number that means nothing.

A launch spike that collapses in week two tells you nothing and earns you little. What matters is whether the book keeps selling in month six, and that comes from the dull fundamentals: accurate targeting, a cover that converts, reviews, and ads that are actually profitable.

We report what we spent and what it returned, every week, including the weeks it did not work.

What is included

  • Launch-week plan with a realistic, modelled sales target
  • Amazon Ads account setup, keyword research and ongoing management
  • Review generation through legitimate ARC reader programmes
  • Email list building with a lead magnet drawn from the book
  • Podcast and press outreach to a researched contact list
  • Social content kit: quote cards, audiograms and teaser video
  • Weekly reporting on spend, sales and return
