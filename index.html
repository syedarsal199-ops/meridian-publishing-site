How it works

From a shoebox of
notes to a finished
book in your hands.

No stage begins until you have signed off on the one before it. You always know where the book is, and you never pay for work you have not approved.

Book your consultation
01 / 05
01

The conversation

A free 30-minute call. You tell us what the book is, who it is for, and when you need it. We tell you honestly whether we are the right house for it — sometimes we are not.

02

Outline & voice sample

Your writer builds a chapter-by-chapter outline and drafts a sample passage in your voice. You read it before any full chapter is written. If the voice is wrong, we change writers, not your expectations.

03

Chapters, weekly

Drafts arrive on a fixed schedule with recorded interviews behind each one. You comment, we revise, and the next chapter does not start until the current one is approved.

04

Edit, design, typeset

Developmental and line editing, then a cover built to your genre’s conventions and an interior typeset for both print and ebook.

05

Publish & launch

ISBNs, metadata, categories, and upload across every major retailer — then, if you want it, a launch campaign built to put the book in front of actual readers.

Cover design, genre by genre.

Sample covers produced in-house. Your book is designed from scratch for its own market — never from a template.

Our promise

Three things we
put in writing.

Not aspirations. Clauses — the same ones that appear in every agreement we sign.

  1. I

    You own the book, completely.

    Copyright, royalties and the final word on every sentence stay with you. We sign an NDA before you send us anything, and our name appears nowhere unless you want it to.

    Rights · Confidentiality
  2. II

    You approve before you pay.

    Work moves in stages, and no stage is billed until you have signed off on the one before it. If the writer’s voice is wrong, we change the writer — not your expectations.

    Payment · Revisions
  3. III

    We will tell you no.

    If your book is not a fit for us, or the idea needs rethinking before a word is written, we say so on the first call. A free consultation that ends in honest advice still costs you nothing.

    Honesty · No obligation

Why authors choose us

Four things most
houses will not do.

Every publishing company says it cares about your book. These are the four operational differences you can actually hold us to — and the questions worth asking whoever else you are speaking to.

01

Genre-matched writers

A memoir writer and a business-book writer are different professionals with different training. Your project goes to someone who writes your genre — never to whoever happens to be free that week.

Ask elsewhere: “Who specifically will write this, and what have they written?”
02

One project manager

A single named person owns your timeline from the first call to the retail upload. You are never forwarded between departments, and you never explain your book twice.

Ask elsewhere: “Who is my point of contact after I pay?”
03

Honest scoping

If your idea needs to be narrower, or the book should be 40,000 words instead of 80,000, we say so before you pay — not after, when the extra words are billable.

Ask elsewhere: “What happens if the book runs longer than quoted?”
04

Built to sell

Covers, metadata, categories and keywords are chosen against live retail data rather than taste alone. A beautiful cover in the wrong category still does not sell.

Ask elsewhere: “How do you choose my categories and keywords?”
