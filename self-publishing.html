Self & Amazon Publishing

Publishing a book is mostly paperwork and metadata, and both are easy to get wrong in ways that quietly cost you readers for years.

The approach

Your categories and keywords decide who ever sees the book. Most authors guess at them.

Amazon shows your book to people browsing specific categories and searching specific phrases. Choose categories that are too broad and you are invisible; choose well and a modest sales figure can make you a category bestseller. This is research, not intuition.

We also register everything in your name. Some services put their own ISBN on your book, which quietly makes them the publisher of record. We do not do that.

What is included

  • ISBN registration in your name, as publisher of record
  • Category research including hidden Amazon subcategories
  • Ten researched keyword strings, not guesses
  • Sales description written and formatted for retail
  • Author Central and author bio setup
  • Distribution to KDP, IngramSpark, Apple Books, Kobo and more
  • Print proof ordered and checked before you go live
  • Pricing strategy for each territory
