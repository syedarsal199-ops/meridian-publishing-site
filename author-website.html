The approach

An author site has one real job, and it is not looking impressive.

It is capturing email addresses. Everything else — the bio, the press page, the pretty header — exists to support the moment a reader decides they want to hear when the next book is out.

We build fast, accessible sites with a clear path from landing to subscription, and hand over every credential so you are never locked in.

What is included

  • Custom design matched to your book’s visual identity
  • Email capture with a lead magnet drawn from the book
  • Individual book pages with links to every retailer
  • Press kit page with downloadable author photos and bios
  • Blog or journal set up for ongoing SEO
  • Technical SEO, schema markup and sitemap
  • Full handover of domain, hosting and mailing-list accounts
