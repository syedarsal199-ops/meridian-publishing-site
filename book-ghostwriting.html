The approach

A ghostwriter is not a typist. They are a structural engineer for your story.

The difference between a manuscript that reads well and one that reads like a transcript is structure. Our writers spend the first two weeks not writing at all — they interview you, map the arc, and decide what the book is actually about, which is often not what you first said it was.

Only then does drafting begin. You receive chapters weekly, each built from recorded conversations, so what arrives sounds like you on your most articulate day.

What is included

  • A writer matched to your genre, not whoever is free
  • Structured interview programme, recorded and transcribed
  • Chapter-by-chapter outline approved before drafting
  • A voice sample written before you commit to the full book
  • Two full revision rounds per chapter
  • NDA signed by every person who touches the manuscript
  • You are credited as sole author; we are never named

The process

How this
actually runs.

Five stages, each gated by your approval. You will always know what happens next and what it costs.

Start the conversation
01

Discovery call

Thirty minutes to establish what the book is, who reads it, and whether we are the right house. We turn down projects that are not a fit.

02

Writer matching

We put forward one or two writers with relevant published work. You read samples and choose.

03

Outline and voice test

A chapter plan plus one sample passage in your voice. This is the checkpoint where mismatches surface cheaply.

04

Interviews and drafting

Weekly recorded sessions feed weekly chapter deliveries. You comment inline, your writer revises.

05

Full manuscript handover

A complete, revised manuscript ready for the editorial stage — yours to take anywhere.
