FAQ

Pricing Questions, Answered

Do I own the rights to my book?

+

Yes. You retain 100% of the copyright and royalties on every project, and every ghostwriter signs an NDA and work-for-hire agreement.

How long does a full book take?

+

Most full-length books take 10–16 weeks from kickoff to a finished, edited manuscript, with publishing adding 2–4 more weeks.

Is there a payment plan?

+

Yes. Projects are typically split into 3–4 milestone payments tied to outline approval, draft delivery, and final publishing.

What if I'm not happy with a chapter?

+

Every package includes structured revision rounds. We don't move to the next chapter until you approve the current one.
