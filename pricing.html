Pricing questions

Before you
ask us.

The questions that come up on almost every consultation call.

Because word count, research depth and timeline genuinely change the work. A 30,000-word guide built from your existing blog posts is a different job from a 90,000-word memoir needing thirty hours of interviews. Every quote is itemised so you can see exactly what drives the figure.

Yes, at every tier. You hold the copyright, the ISBN, the retail accounts and 100% of royalties. We never take a percentage of what the book earns.

Projects are split into three to four milestone payments tied to deliverables you have approved — typically outline approval, half the manuscript, full manuscript, and publication.

Every package includes two revision rounds per chapter, and we do not start the next chapter until the current one is approved. If the voice is wrong at the sample stage we change writers at no cost.

Third-party costs are passed through at cost and quoted separately: ISBN purchase if you want your own block, premium stock photography or original illustration, print runs, and advertising spend. We never mark these up.

Yes. Roughly a third of our clients arrive with a finished draft and need only editing, design and publishing. We will quote exactly those stages.
