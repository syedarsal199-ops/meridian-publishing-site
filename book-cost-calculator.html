    Why ghostwriting quotes differ so much

    A 60,000-word book is roughly 300 hours of skilled work: interviews, transcription, structural planning, drafting, two editing passes and revisions. Quotes below about $5,000 for a full-length book usually mean one of three things — the work is offshored to writers paid a few cents a word, the draft is largely AI-generated and lightly edited, or the low number is an opening figure that grows once you have signed.

    The honest version is that you are buying a professional's time. Our rates work out around $0.12 to $0.18 per word for ghostwriting, which is the going rate for writers who have published books you would recognise. What varies is how much research the book needs and how much of it you have already written — which is exactly what the calculator above is adjusting for.
